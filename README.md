### 1. Project Description

This project aims to develop an AI-powered web platform that helps users explore mental health conditions through educational information, interactive visualizations, and lived-experience materials.

### 2. Problem Statement

Mental health conditions are often misunderstood, and stigma can negatively affect people experiencing them. The platform aims to improve users’ understanding of these conditions and the experiences of people living with them.

#### Target users:

- People interested in learning about mental health.
- People seeking to better understand their own or others’ experiences.
- Students and other users interested in mental health education.

#### Expected outcomes:

- An interactive web platform with a catalog of mental health conditions.
- Educational pages and visual materials.
- An AI-powered assistant for navigating information.
- A user-friendly interface that supports mental health awareness.
